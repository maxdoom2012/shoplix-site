Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does Shoplix prevent people from buying the same item twice?

Shoplix features a live item claiming system: tap any product avatar slot, and your face or initials appear on it instantly across everyone’s phone (“I’ll grab it”). In Event Lists, the app also warns you immediately if someone attempts to add an item that is already on the list.

How do Family Lists differ from Event Lists?

In a continuous Family List (daily groceries, pharmacy), items soft-delete and disappear when checked off, keeping the list clean. In an Event List (BBQ party, holiday trip), purchased items stay visible in a dedicated completed section with an audit trail and spend breakdown per participant.

Can I use Shoplix without creating an account?

Yes. Shoplix operates on an Anonymous-First architecture powered by device-based tokens. You can install the app and start sharing lists immediately with zero registration, passwords, or personal data collection. Apple or Google sign-in is completely optional.

Does Shoplix work offline without cell reception?

Yes. All lists and changes are saved locally in SQLite on your device first. You can browse, add, and check off items deep inside a supermarket basement without a connection. As soon as connectivity returns, your changes sync smoothly with automatic 409 conflict resolution.
